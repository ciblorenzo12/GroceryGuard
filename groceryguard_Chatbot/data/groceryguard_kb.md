## What is GroceryGuard?
GroceryGuard is a task-oriented chatbot that helps scan ingredient labels and explain ingredients in plain language.

## What does “flagged ingredient” mean?
It means the ingredient matched a watchlist item (or an alias). It’s not a diagnosis—just a heads-up so you can decide.

## What should I do if I have allergies?
Always follow medical guidance. GroceryGuard can help you spot ingredients, but it’s not a medical authority.
